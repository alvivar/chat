# The Resonance Laws

## Rule 1: The Law of Sympathetic Connection
All matter and energy exists in a state of potential resonance with other matter and energy that shares similar fundamental properties (composition, origin, or exposure to shared experiences). The strength of this connection decreases with physical distance and increases with similarity.

## Rule 2: The Law of Resonant Manipulation
Individuals can influence connected objects or energies by manipulating their resonant counterpart, but doing so creates an equal and opposite effect on the manipulator. The more dramatic the manipulation, the more severe the backlash.

## Rule 3: The Law of Resonant Decay
All resonant connections naturally weaken over time unless actively maintained through ritual, proximity, or renewed shared experience. Connections that are forced beyond their natural strength will snap catastrophically, severing all future resonance between those elements.

## Rule 4: The Law of Harmonic Interference
When multiple resonant connections intersect or conflict, they create unpredictable interference patterns. These can amplify effects, cancel them out entirely, or create entirely new phenomena that follow none of the standard rules.

## Rule 5: The Law of Resonant Inheritance
Objects, places, or beings can inherit resonant connections from their creators, previous owners, or locations where significant events occurred. These inherited connections are typically weaker than direct connections but can accumulate and strengthen over generations.

---

*These laws create a magic system based on connection and consequence, where power comes with inherent risks and the world's history literally shapes its present capabilities.*
