# Character Profiles

## 1. Mira Thornwick - The Resonance Archaeologist

**Age:** 34  
**Occupation:** Historical Investigator & Artifact Specialist

### Personality & Motivations
Mira is methodical, patient, and deeply empathetic—traits that serve her well when handling objects steeped in centuries of accumulated resonance. She's driven by an insatiable curiosity about the past and a genuine desire to help people understand their inherited burdens. However, she struggles with perfectionism and often takes on too much emotional weight from the objects she studies.

### Interaction with the System
- **Primary Focus:** Rule 5 (Resonant Inheritance) - Mira specializes in tracing the accumulated resonant connections of ancient artifacts and locations
- **Technique:** She uses careful ritual maintenance (Rule 3) to preserve weak inherited connections while investigating their origins
- **Limitation:** Her work often creates interference patterns (Rule 4) when multiple historical connections overlap, leading to unpredictable visions or phantom experiences

### Goals & Conflicts
**Primary Goal:** To establish a comprehensive archive of resonant inheritance patterns to help families understand why certain bloodlines are cursed or blessed

**Central Conflict:** She's discovered that her own family's ancestral home contains a catastrophically severed connection (Rule 3) from three generations ago—something her grandfather did that broke their lineage's connection to their protective spirits. Now she must decide whether to attempt the dangerous process of reforging that connection or let her family line remain vulnerable.

### Key Relationships
- **Kael Brightforge:** Professional partnership that's grown into deep friendship; she provides historical context for his work while he helps her understand the technical aspects of resonant manipulation
- **The Ashford Family:** Her current clients, whose inherited mansion is driving family members to madness through overlapping resonant echoes from multiple tragic deaths

## 2. Kael Brightforge - The Resonant Smith

**Age:** 28  
**Occupation:** Craftsman & Resonant Artificer

### Personality & Motivations
Kael is bold, innovative, and somewhat reckless—qualities that make him both a brilliant artificer and a walking collection of scars from resonant backlash. He's motivated by the desire to push the boundaries of what's possible with resonant manipulation, often ignoring the safer, traditional methods. His cheerful exterior masks a deep fear of being ordinary.

### Interaction with the System
- **Primary Focus:** Rule 2 (Resonant Manipulation) - Kael crafts items by manipulating their resonant connections during creation
- **Technique:** He deliberately creates sympathetic connections (Rule 1) between his tools and materials, then manipulates these connections to imbue objects with specific properties
- **Consequence:** Every dramatic manipulation leaves him with physical and emotional scars—burns that mirror the heat he puts into metal, aches that echo the stress he places on materials

### Goals & Conflicts
**Primary Goal:** To create a "resonant amplifier"—a device that could strengthen weak connections without the risk of catastrophic severance

**Central Conflict:** His latest project requires him to work with materials connected to a disaster site where dozens died. The resonant interference (Rule 4) from so many overlapping death-connections is creating increasingly dangerous and unpredictable effects in his workshop. He's torn between abandoning the project and risking not just his own life, but potentially creating a resonant cascade that could affect the entire district.

### Key Relationships
- **Mira Thornwick:** His closest friend and professional partner; she grounds his reckless tendencies while he encourages her to take necessary risks
- **Master Jorik:** His former teacher, who was permanently changed by a resonant backlash and now serves as both mentor and cautionary tale

## 3. Sera Nightwhisper - The Connection Broker

**Age:** 42  
**Occupation:** Mediator & Resonant Consultant

### Personality & Motivations
Sera is calculating, diplomatic, and deeply pragmatic. She has an almost supernatural ability to read people and understand the hidden connections that bind them. Her motivation is complex—part genuine desire to help people navigate their resonant entanglements, part fascination with the power that comes from controlling information about connections. She's haunted by a past decision that severed her from her own family.

### Interaction with the System
- **Primary Focus:** Rule 4 (Harmonic Interference) - Sera specializes in managing and resolving conflicts between multiple resonant connections
- **Technique:** She uses her knowledge of sympathetic connections (Rule 1) to predict and prevent dangerous interference patterns, often by carefully weakening or redirecting connections before they clash
- **Unique Ability:** Through years of practice, she's learned to sense the "resonant landscape" around people—seeing their connections as a kind of visible network

### Goals & Conflicts
**Primary Goal:** To prevent a brewing "resonant war" between two major families whose inherited connections are beginning to create city-wide interference patterns

**Central Conflict:** Sera discovered that resolving the family conflict requires her to reconnect with her own severed family line—something she's avoided for fifteen years. The process would require her to confront the truth about why she broke those connections and risk experiencing the collective pain she's been hiding from. However, without her family's inherited knowledge of large-scale resonant management, she may not be able to prevent a catastrophe that could affect thousands.

### Key Relationships
- **Both Mira and Kael:** She serves as a client and occasional employer, bringing them cases while using their expertise to better understand the technical aspects of the problems she mediates
- **The Valdris and Thorne Families:** The feuding bloodlines whose escalating resonant conflict threatens the city; she's been working with both sides while hiding her own connections to the original incident that started their feud
- **Her estranged sister Elena:** A connection she severed years ago, but whose inherited knowledge of their family's resonant techniques may be the key to preventing disaster

### Interconnected Dynamics
The three characters form a natural triangle of expertise and mutual dependence:
- Mira provides historical context and inheritance analysis
- Kael offers technical innovation and practical application  
- Sera manages the human elements and systemic conflicts

Their individual conflicts are beginning to converge around a larger threat that will require all three to overcome their personal limitations and work together.
